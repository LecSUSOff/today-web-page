Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Today mobile application and any related services operated by Alexey Zhukovsky (referred to as the "Service Provider", "we", "us", or "our").

1. Information Collection and Use

To provide a functional experience, synchronize user content across devices, and manage in-app features, the Service Provider collects personal and usage information. This information includes:

  • Account Identification: Email address, Username, Password, User ID / Account Identifier, and Profile photos.
  • User Content: Journal entries, memory titles, notes, and dates.
  • In-App Activity & Customization: In-app virtual currency (Sparks balance), activity/streak data, unlocked theme accent colors, and app preferences (language selection, dark/light theme, app lock state).
  • Automatically Collected Telemetry: Internet Protocol (IP) address, mobile device operating system, app usage duration, diagnostic logs, and interaction timestamps.

2. Account and Data Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

If you create an account within the Application, you have the right to permanently delete your account and all associated personal data at any time.

  • In-App Deletion: You can initiate account deletion directly within the Application's settings menu.
  • Web Deletion Request: If you cannot access the Application, you can request account and data deletion by contacting us at lecsusoff@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

Effect of Deletion: Upon receiving and verifying an account deletion request, we permanently remove your account profile, personal identification, journal entries, notes, and virtual items from our active databases. Any non-identifiable aggregated data may be retained for analytical purposes.

Privacy

Updated: June 20, 2026

I built Today because I believe your history should belong to you. I don't sell your data, I don't show you ads, and I don't track your location. This page explains exactly where your data goes.

The TodayID

I made the TodayID mandatory because losing your journal is a nightmare. By creating an ID, your memories are tied to your email. If you switch phones or delete the app, your history is still safe.

Offline-First: The app stores everything on your phone first. It works perfectly without internet. When you do have a signal, it syncs your entries to the cloud so you have a backup.

Artificial Intelligence

I use Hugging Face to power features like "Voice-to-Memory" and title generation.

  • When you record a note, the audio is sent to an API to be turned into text.
  • Your data is processed, not stored. It is used to perform the task and then it is gone.
  • Your entries are never used to "teach" or train global AI models.

Infrastructure

I use Supabase to handle the heavy lifting of accounts and database storage. They are industry leaders in security. Your data is encrypted while it's moving and while it's sitting on the server.

Control

You own everything you write. You can edit or delete any entry inside the app. If you want to delete your entire TodayID and every byte of data I have on the servers, just email me.

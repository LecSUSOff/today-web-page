Privacy

Updated: June 20, 2026

I built Today because I believe your history should belong to you. I don't sell your data, I don't show you ads, and I don't track your location. This page explains exactly where your data goes.

The TodayID

I made the TodayID mandatory because losing your journal is a nightmare. By creating an ID, your memories are tied to your email. If you switch phones or delete the app, your history is still safe.

Offline-First: The app stores everything on your phone first. It works perfectly without an internet connection. When you do have a signal, it syncs your entries securely to the cloud so you always have a backup.

Artificial Intelligence

I use Hugging Face APIs to power features like "Voice-to-Memory" and automated title generation.

  • When you record a note, the audio is securely sent to an API to be converted into text.
  • Your data is processed, not stored. It is strictly used to perform the requested task and is immediately erased.
  • Your private journal entries are never used to "teach" or train global AI models.

Infrastructure

I use Supabase to handle user accounts and database storage. They are industry leaders in security infrastructure. Your data is encrypted both while moving (in transit) and while sitting securely on the server (at rest).

Control

You own everything you write. You can completely edit or delete any entry inside the app at any time. If you want to delete your entire TodayID account and wipe every single byte of your data from the servers permanently, just email me.
